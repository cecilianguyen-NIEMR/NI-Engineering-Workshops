NI Engineering Workshops
========================

The NI Engineering Workshops is a hands-on learning program designed to help students develop practical engineering skills through real-world test and measurement applications.
 
This site serves as the online version of our workshops, allowing you to learn at your own pace and revisit materials whenever you need them. Each level builds on the concepts from previous workshops and combines engineering theory with hands-on activities that mirror real-world engineering workflows.
 
Since this is an online experience, we have provided:
 
* Step-by-step workshop manuals
* Sample code for LabVIEW and Python
* Simulated hardware and datasets that allow you to follow along without access to physical equipment
* Additional resources and reference materials to support your learning
 
As you progress through each level, you'll gain experience with engineering concepts such as measurement systems, data acquisition, analysis, automation, and test engineering fundamentals.
 
Certification
-------------
 
At the end of each workshop, you will be asked to complete a short feedback survey. The survey helps us improve the program and better understand your learning experience.
 
Students who complete the workshop activities and submit the feedback survey will be eligible to receive the corresponding certificate of completion for that level.
 
We hope this program helps you build confidence in your technical abilities, strengthen your engineering mindset, and develop skills that can be applied across a wide range of industries.
 
Ready to get started? Select a workshop level from the navigation menu and begin your journey.
